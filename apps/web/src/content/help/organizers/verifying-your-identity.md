# Verifying your identity

People who run events or places on Abonten verify who they are, so that when something goes wrong, such as a fraud report or an emergency at an event, Abonten knows who is responsible. It takes a few minutes: a photo of a government ID and a short selfie.

> **Not available to everyone yet.** Identity verification is being tried by Abonten staff. This page describes how it works once it opens.

## When you need it

Abonten may ask you to verify before you can:

- add an account for payouts, or be paid;
- have a place you claimed handed over to you;
- get a Verified badge for your organizer profile or your place;
- sell tickets (free events stay open to everyone).

The identity page tells you which of these apply to you.

## How to verify

1. Open **Settings › Identity verification** on the website or in the app.
2. Choose your document. In Ghana you can use your **Ghana Card**, a **passport**, a **driver's licence** or a **voter ID**.
3. Tick the box to agree, then press **Start the check**.
4. You go to the secure page of our verification partner, **Smile ID**. Take a photo of your document and a short selfie. Use good light, keep the whole document in the frame, and don't use filters.
5. Come back to Abonten. Most checks are decided automatically; we tell you the result.

A Ghana Card is checked against the National Identification Authority's record. Other documents are checked from the document itself.

## What Abonten keeps

Abonten keeps the name and date of birth on your document, the last four characters of its number and when it expires, plus the result of the check. Your photos stay with Smile ID: Abonten never stores them. Nobody else on Abonten sees your details; Abonten staff look at them only to settle a problem, and every look is recorded. More in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

Other people see an **"ID checked"** mark beside your name. It means Abonten checked a government ID and a live selfie. It does not mean Abonten vouches for you, your events or your places.

## If the check doesn't pass

The identity page tells you why and what to do:

- **Your document has expired**: use one that is still valid.
- **We couldn't read your document**: try again with good light and the whole document in the frame.
- **Your selfie doesn't match** or **wasn't taken live**: try again in good light, facing the camera, without filters or glasses.
- **The document is already on another Abonten account**: contact Abonten support.
- **Under 18**: you must be at least 18 to verify.

You can try three times a day. If a check is being reviewed by someone at Abonten, you'll get a notice when it's done.

## How long it lasts

A check lasts three years, or until your document expires if that is sooner. In the last two months, the identity page lets you verify again.
