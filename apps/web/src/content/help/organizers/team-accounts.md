# Team accounts

A team account lets several people run one organizer or one place together. The team has its own name, @username, events, places, followers, messages and money. Each member signs in with their own Abonten account and acts for the team with a role. Nobody shares a password.

> **Opening to businesses one by one.** Abonten switches team accounts on for each business before it starts. See the [step-by-step guide](/teams).

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do for the team |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything: members and roles, the team's profile, payout account, payouts, refunds, featuring, events, places, check-in, messages, Spotlight, reviews and verification |
| Finance | Earnings and payouts, refunds, featuring the team's events and places |
| Manager | Events, places, promo codes, bookings, the attendee list and check-in |
| Content and messages | Messages, Spotlight and Stories, replies to reviews |
| Door staff | Scanning tickets at the door |
| Front desk | Bookings and the place's visit QR |

A team always has at least one Owner. An Owner can limit Door staff and Front desk members to some of the team's places, for example one branch of a chain.

## Making a team

Go to **Settings › Teams** on the website or in the app, choose **Make a team**, and give it a name and an @username. If you already run events or places on your own account, you can choose **Make your organizer account a team** instead: the team keeps your @username, events, places, followers and organizer money, and you get a new @username for yourself. Your tickets, credit and private messages stay yours.

Teams are run by adults: you confirm you are 18 or older when you make or join a team. Once Abonten publishes terms for team accounts, you also accept them for the team and confirm you may act for it.

## Inviting people

Owners invite people by their @username or their phone number and choose their role. The invitation waits for them for a week; they accept or decline it under **Settings › Teams**. Someone without an Abonten account gets a text and finds the invitation once they sign up with that number.

## Acting for the team

Choose the team under **Managing as** to work on its events, places, money or messages. Everything you do is recorded in the team's activity with your name, which every Owner can see. Switch back to your own account the same way.

## The team's money

Only Owners and Finance members can ask for the team's payouts, and only Owners can change where the money goes. Changing the payout account needs a recent sign-in, every Owner is told, and the first payout to a new account waits 48 hours. When Abonten requires ID checks for payouts, the Owner or Finance member who asks must have passed one. When team terms are published, payouts wait until one of the team's Owners has accepted them.

## Promoters from your own team

People in your team do not earn rewards or promoter commission on your own events, unless an Owner adds them as a promoter of an event on its **Promotion** tab. They then earn your commission on that event, never Abonten's own referral rewards.

## Badges

A team that passes Abonten's business check shows **Verified business**. A team shows **ID checked** when at least one of its Owners has passed an ID check; it never says which one. Neither badge means Abonten vouches for the team, its events or its places.

## Leaving and removing

Members can leave a team at any time, except the last Owner, who names another Owner first. Owners can remove members. If a member's account is closed, they leave every team; a team left with no Owner is paused until Abonten appoints one. If there is a dispute about who controls a team, contact support.
