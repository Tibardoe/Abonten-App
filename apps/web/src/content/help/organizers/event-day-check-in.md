# Event day — attendees and check-in

## The attendee list

Open your event › **Attendees** (web: Manage › Events › event › Insights; app: Organizer › Events › event › Attendees). It lists every ticket holder with their ticket type and status, and lets you search by name.

Attendee names and contact details are shared with you **only to run this event**. Keep them confidential; do not export them for marketing or share them with third parties (see the Terms and Conditions).

## Checking people in

**From the list (web and app):** find the attendee and tap **Check in**. Their ticket becomes *used*. Tap again to undo if you made a mistake.

**By scanning (app only):** on the attendee screen choose **Scan**. Allow camera access the first time. Point the camera at the attendee's QR code; the app reads the ticket code and checks them in, showing a clear success or error message. You can also type or paste a `TKT-` code.

## What the scanner tells you

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Checked in | Valid ticket, first use |
| Already used | This ticket was checked in before — ask to see the buyer's app; the code may have been shared |
| Cancelled / not found | The ticket was cancelled or is not for this event |

## Free events

Registrations are tickets too and are checked in the same way.

## No internet at the venue?

Check-in needs a connection to record the ticket as used. Where signal is poor, a phone hotspot usually works; as a fallback, record names manually and check them in afterwards from the list.

## After the event

Attendees who were checked in can leave a review once the event ends. Earnings become available for payout 48 hours after the event's last date — see [Finance, payouts and settlement](/help/organizers/finance-payouts-and-settlement).
