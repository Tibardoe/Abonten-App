# Cancelling an event

Cancelling is **final and cannot be undone**. Consider editing the date or venue instead — buyers keep their tickets when you edit.

## How to cancel

Open the event under Manage › Events (web) or Organizer › Events (app) and choose **Cancel event**. The confirmation shows how many **paid tickets**, **free registrations** and **attendees** are affected before you confirm.

## What Abonten does when you confirm

1. Marks the event **cancelled**; it disappears from discovery and sales stop.
2. Cancels every ticket and registration; attendees see *Cancelled by organizer*.
3. **Requests a refund of the ticket price for every paid ticket** from Paystack. Buyers get their ticket price back to the payment method they used; Abonten's service fee is not refunded to them.
4. Notifies every attendee in the app, and paid attendees by email. The notice says a refund is *processing* — it is confirmed by Paystack later.
5. Reverses the pending earnings for those tickets on your ledger.

If a refund request fails (for example the payment provider was unavailable), the buyer sees a *Retry refund* option, and Abonten's finance team can re-request it.

## Your money

Earnings from a cancelled event are reversed. If part of an earlier payout already included them, your available balance can go negative and is netted against future earnings.

## Promotions

A paid promotion on a cancelled event is not refunded.

## Postponing instead

Abonten has no "postponed" state. Edit the event's dates and message attendees; they keep their tickets. If some attendees cannot make the new date, they can cancel their own ticket and receive the ticket price back.

## Communicating

Use **Message** from the event to reach attendees who have contacted you, and post the reason in the event description before cancelling so people who look the event up understand what happened.
