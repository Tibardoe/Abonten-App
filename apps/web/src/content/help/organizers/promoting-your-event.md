# Promoting your event

## What promotion does

A promoted event is **featured**: it appears in the featured carousel on the home and explore pages for your location and is labelled as promoted. Promotion does not change search ranking rules beyond the featured placement.

## Buy a promotion

1. Open your event › **Promote** (web: Manage › Events › event; app: Organizer › Events › event › Promote).
2. Choose a tier — durations run from 24 hours to one month; each tier shows its price before you pay.
3. Pay with a card, mobile money or a saved method. Where the Abonten Rewards programme is live and you hold promotion credit, checkout shows how much credit is applied and how much cash remains.
4. The promotion **starts when payment is confirmed** and runs for the purchased duration. Your active promotions appear under the event and in your transaction history.

## Rules

- Promotions are not refundable once started, except where Abonten failed to deliver them.
- A listing that has been **restricted**, **hidden** or **removed** for breaking the rules cannot be promoted, and a promotion on a listing later removed is not refunded.
- A promotion on an event you cancel is not refunded.

## Promoter commission (the other kind of promotion)

Separate from paid featuring, you can let other people promote your event for a share of each sale — see [Selling tickets, promo codes and promoter commission](/help/organizers/selling-tickets-and-promo-codes).
