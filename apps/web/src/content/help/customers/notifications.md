# Notifications

## In the app and on the web

The bell icon (web header) and **Account › Notifications** (app) list your notifications: ticket confirmations, messages, booking updates, review replies, event cancellations, claim decisions, rewards updates and more. Tap one to open the related item; mark one or all as read.

## Push notifications

When you sign in on the app it asks permission to send push notifications. Each device you sign in on is registered separately; signing out removes that device. You can turn push notifications off in your phone's settings for the Abonten app. Where your browser supports it, the website can send them too: turn them on in Settings › Notifications, and off again there or in your browser.

Event **reminders** you set in the app are sent by Abonten to each device you are signed in on, with a local alarm on the phone as a backup.

## Alerts and picks

- **Notify me** on an organizer, a place or an event you have a ticket for sends you an alert when they post a new event. It is private: nobody is told you turned it on.
- **Picks** are suggestions based on what you have bought, saved and followed: at most one a day and three a week, by push and in your notifications list, never urgent.
- **Settings › Alerts and picks** lists what you follow, lets you stop any of it, and can **pause alerts and picks for two weeks** (tickets and payments still reach you). **Email me picks and alerts** is off unless you turn it on, and every such email has an unsubscribe link.

## Emails

Abonten emails you when:

- you buy tickets (your tickets are attached),
- an organizer cancels an event you have a paid ticket for,
- you sign in with an email code,
- (when the rewards programme is available) Abonten Credit is ready for you to use.

**Rewards emails** can be turned off on the Rewards page or with the *Unsubscribe* link in any rewards email; the link works without signing in and nothing changes until you press the button on the page. Ticket, cancellation and sign-in emails are part of the service and cannot be turned off.

Abonten sends no marketing newsletters. Picks and alerts by email arrive only if you turned them on.

## Not receiving something?

- Check the notifications list in the app or web first — pushes and emails are copies of what is there.
- **Push:** make sure notifications are allowed for Abonten in your phone settings and that you are signed in on that phone.
- **Email:** check spam, and check the address on Settings › Security is correct.
- Still missing? Contact support.
