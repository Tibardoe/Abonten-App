# Finding events and places

## Choose where you are exploring

Abonten shows you events and places for a location. On the web, the location appears in the header and on the home page; in the app it is at the top of the Home tab. You can:

- let the app use your **device location** (you will be asked for permission once), or
- type a town or area and pick it from the suggestions.

The app remembers the last location you chose.

## Browse

- **Home / Explore** shows featured (promoted) listings, events happening soon, and places by category.
- **Events** lists upcoming events for your location, with filters for category, date range, price (free or paid), distance and sort order.
- **Places** groups venues, restaurants, bars and other businesses by category. A place page shows opening hours (and whether it is open right now), services, photos, contact buttons, a map and upcoming events there.
- **Around you** (web) and **Nearby** sorting use your device location to order results by distance.

Listings that Abonten has hidden or removed for breaking the rules never appear in results.

## Search

Type in the search box to see suggestions as you go. Search results list matching events; place suggestions appear in the suggestion list. Your last eight searches are kept on your device for quick access.

## The map

Event and place lists have a **map view** with a pin for each listing; tap a pin to see a card and open the listing. Maps need an internet connection; on Android the map is provided by Google.

## Event pages

An event page shows the flyer, organizer, date(s) and time, venue with a map, description, tags, ticket types and prices, ratings and reviews, and similar events. Buttons let you **buy tickets or register**, **save** the event, **share** it, **message the organizer**, and **report** it.

Multi-date events show each occurrence; you choose the date when you buy.

## Place pages

A place page shows photos, verified/claimed badges, opening hours, services, contact options (call, WhatsApp, website, directions), reviews and upcoming events at that place. You can **save** the place, **share** it, **request a booking**, **message the place**, and **report** it. If you own the business and the listing is not yours, use **claim this place** — see [Claiming and verifying a place](/help/place-owners/claiming-and-verifying-a-place).

## Saving and sharing

- **Save** (heart) an event or place to find it again under your profile's *Favourites* tab. Favourites are private to you.
- **Share** copies or sends a link. On the app the share sheet offers your installed apps, including WhatsApp. Links open the listing on the web, and in the app if it is installed.

## Reminders (app only)

In the app you can set a reminder for an event from its page. Reminders are stored on your phone and shown as local notifications; they are not synced to other devices.

## Something wrong with a listing?

Use **Report** on the event or place, pick the reason (spam, fraud or scam, misleading, harassment, inappropriate, fake listing, safety, copyright, impersonation, other), describe the problem and attach a screenshot if you have one. Reports go to Abonten's moderation team.
