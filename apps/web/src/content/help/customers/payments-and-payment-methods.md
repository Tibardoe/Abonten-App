# Payments and payment methods

Payments on Abonten are processed by **Paystack**. Prices are in Ghana cedis.

## Ways to pay

- **Card** — enter your card in Paystack's secure pop-up. Abonten never sees your full card number or PIN.
- **Mobile money** — choose your network and number. Depending on the network you either approve a prompt on your phone or enter a one-time code in the app.
- **Saved method** — pick a card or wallet you saved earlier and pay in one step.

## Your wallet (saved payment methods)

**Web:** side menu › Wallet. **App:** Account › Wallet.

- **Add a card.** Paystack verifies the card with a **GHS 1.00 charge that is refunded immediately** and gives Abonten a reusable reference. Abonten stores only that reference plus the brand, last four digits and expiry so you can recognise the card.
- **Add a mobile-money wallet.** Saved as display details (network, number, label). You still confirm each payment on your phone.
- Set one method as **default**, or **remove** a method at any time. Removing a method does not affect past payments or refunds.

Your wallet is separate from checkout: you can manage methods without having anything in your basket.

## Transaction history

**Web:** side menu › Transactions. **App:** Account › Transactions.

The overview shows totals for the period you pick (total, successful, pending, failed, tickets purchased, amount spent). The history lists every ticket purchase with its status; open one to see the ticket price, service fee and total paid, the payment reference, and refund status.

Statuses you may see:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending | Checkout started, not yet paid |
| Successful | Paid and tickets issued |
| Failed | Payment declined or abandoned |
| Refund pending | A refund has been requested from Paystack |
| Refunded | Paystack confirmed the refund |

## Receipts

A confirmation email with your tickets is sent when a purchase completes (if your account has an email). You can also download or share a ticket as a PDF from the ticket itself.

## Problems

- **Charged but no ticket** — see [Buying tickets](/help/customers/tickets-and-checkout) ("When the payment takes a moment"). Use Retry, then contact support if it is still missing; include the time and amount.
- **Charged twice** — Abonten prevents double charges for one checkout. If your statement shows two charges, one is usually a pending authorization that will drop off; if both settle, contact support with both references.
- **Card verification GHS 1.00 not refunded** — Paystack reverses it immediately, but some banks show it for a few days before it disappears.
