# Messaging

## Start a conversation

- **With an organizer** — open the event and choose **Message**. All your messages about that organizer's events go into one conversation.
- **With a place** — open the place and choose **Message**.
- **With Abonten Support** — web: Messages › *Contact Abonten Support*; app: Account › **Help & support**. Support replies arrive in the same conversation and as notifications.

Opening a conversation twice always returns to the same thread.

## Inside a conversation

- Send **text**, **photos**, **files** and (in the app) **voice notes** recorded with the microphone.
- **Reply** to a specific message, add an **emoji reaction**, **copy** text.
- **Edit** or **delete** your own messages.
- Messages that are only emoji show larger.
- Unread counts appear on the Messages tab and in the header.

## Managing your inbox

- **Filters** switch between all conversations, unread, and (for organizers and owners) business conversations.
- **Archive** a conversation to move it out of the main list; find it under *Archived*.
- **Mark as unread** to come back to something later.
- **Block** a participant to stop receiving their messages. Blocking is per conversation participant.

## Your privacy

Only the participants of a conversation can read it. Abonten staff can read a conversation **only** when it is reported, or when it is a support conversation. Moderators can hide a message that breaks the rules; the sender still sees it marked as hidden.

## Report abuse

Use **Report** on a message or on the conversation, choosing *harassment*, *spam*, *inappropriate* or another reason. See [Reporting a problem](/help/customers/reporting-a-problem).

## Notifications

New messages trigger an in-app notification and, in the app, a push notification. See [Notifications](/help/customers/notifications).
