# Rewards and Abonten Credit

> **Availability.** The Abonten Rewards programme is being trialled internally and is **not yet available to the public**. You may not see a Rewards page, and no credit is being granted to public accounts at the moment. This page describes how the programme works when it is switched on for you.

## What Abonten Credit is

Abonten Credit is a promotional balance held in your Abonten account. It can be spent on Abonten — towards tickets or towards promoting an event or place, depending on the scope of each grant — but it is **not money**: it cannot be withdrawn, transferred to someone else, or exchanged outside Abonten. Grants can have an expiry date.

## Ways credit can be earned

| Activity | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Inviting friends** | Share your invite link or code from the Rewards page. When a friend signs up as a new account within the first week of arriving, verifies their phone and buys their first ticket, they may receive a welcome credit and you a reward. |
| **Sharing events** | Share an event with your referral link. If someone buys a ticket through it, a share of that sale may be credited to you after the event takes place and the sale settles. |
| **Loyalty** | Regular ticket buyers may get the service fee on every fifth order (on different events) back as credit. |
| **Organizers and venue owners** | A monthly rebate on ticket sales for settled events, paid as promotion credit. |
| **Promoting an organizer's event** | Organizers may offer a commission to promoters, paid in credit. |
| **Visiting places** | Scanning a place's visit QR code (once per day, at the place) can count towards the owner's rewards. |

Rewards for referrals and sales are **provisional** until the event has happened and the sale has settled. They are reduced or cancelled if the sale is refunded, disputed or reversed. Abonten screens every reward for abuse (self-referral, duplicate accounts, shared devices or payment methods, unusual velocity); rewards flagged for review are decided by Abonten staff.

## Spending credit

When you have spendable credit, checkout shows how much is applied and how much cash remains. Credit scoped to promotions can only be used for featuring, not tickets.

## Your Rewards page

Web: side menu › Rewards. App: Account › Rewards. It shows your balance and its scopes, upcoming expiries, activity history, your invite link, loyalty progress, and the **Email me when credit is ready** switch.

## Leaving Abonten

If you delete your account, any credit is forfeited and pending rewards are cancelled.

## Something looks wrong

If a reward you expected is missing or a balance looks incorrect, contact support with the event or friend involved; staff can see the decision and its reason and can make adjustments where justified.
