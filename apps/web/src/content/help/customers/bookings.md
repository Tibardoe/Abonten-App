# Booking a table or a service at a place

Some places accept **booking requests** through Abonten. A booking is a request to the business, not a paid reservation — Abonten does not take payment for bookings.

## Send a request

1. Open the place and choose **Request a booking**.
2. Pick a service (if the place lists any), the date and time, your party size, and add a note.
3. Send. The owner is notified in the app.

## What happens next

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending | Waiting for the owner |
| Accepted | The owner confirmed it |
| Declined | The owner could not take it |
| Cancelled | You cancelled it |

You are notified when the owner responds. Find your bookings under your profile's **Bookings** tab (web: `/user/<your username>/bookings`) or **Account › Bookings** in the app; they are private to you.

## Cancel a request

Open the booking and choose **Cancel**. You can cancel a pending or accepted booking; please do it as early as you can so the place can plan.

## Talk to the place

Use **Message** on the place page to ask a question before or after booking. You can also call or WhatsApp the place directly if it lists a number — that happens outside Abonten.
