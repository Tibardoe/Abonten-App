# Refunds and cancellations

## Cancelling a ticket you bought

Open the ticket in My Tickets (web) or the Tickets tab (app) and choose **Cancel ticket** while it is still *Active*. Cancelling releases the ticket so someone else can buy it.

## What you get back

- The **ticket price** for the cancelled ticket is refunded to the payment method you paid with.
- The **service fee is not refunded.**
- Free tickets have nothing to refund; they simply move to *Cancelled*.
- If you bought several tickets in one payment, each one you cancel refunds its own ticket price. Paystack refunds are requested per cancelled ticket's share of the charge.

## How long it takes

Cancelling **requests** the refund from Paystack immediately. Your transaction shows **Refund pending** until Paystack confirms it, then **Refunded**. After that, the time it takes to appear on your card or mobile-money balance depends on your bank or network — typically a few days for cards.

If the request fails (for example Paystack was unreachable), your ticket shows **Refund failed** with a **Retry refund** button. Support can also re-request it.

## When the organizer cancels an event

If an organizer cancels, Abonten automatically:

1. cancels every ticket and registration for the event (yours will read *Cancelled by organizer*);
2. requests a refund of the ticket price for every paid ticket, on the same terms as above (the service fee is not refunded);
3. notifies you in the app and, for paid tickets, by email.

You do not need to do anything. If a refund shows *Refund failed*, use *Retry refund* or contact support.

## Bookings at places

A booking request can be cancelled from your profile's *Bookings* tab (web) or Account › Bookings (app) at any time before it takes place. Bookings are free; there is nothing to refund.

## Refunds we cannot give

Abonten cannot refund a ticket because you missed the event, arrived late, disliked it, or the event differed from what you expected — those matters are between you and the organizer, whom you can message from the event page. You can also report the event if you believe it was fraudulent or misleading.

## Disputes with your bank

Please contact us before raising a dispute (chargeback) with your bank — most problems are quicker to fix through support. While a dispute is open, the organizer's payout for that event is held and your account may be paused.
