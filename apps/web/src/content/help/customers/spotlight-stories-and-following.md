# Spotlight, Stories and following

## Spotlight

**Spotlight** is a feed of short vertical videos and photos posted by organizers, place owners and Abonten. Open it from the **Spotlight** tab in the app or from the menu on the website. Choose what to watch:

- **For you** — a mix based on what you watch, buy and follow;
- **Following** — only organizers and places you follow;
- **Nearby** — posts from around the area you are exploring;
- **Happening soon** — posts about events coming up shortly;
- **Trending** — what many people are watching now.

A post can link an event or a place. Its button always shows the listing's current state, such as *View event*, *Sold out* or *Event cancelled*.

On a post you can **like** it, **comment** (and reply to a comment), **save** it to find it later under *Saved Spotlights* on your profile, **share** it, or choose **Not interested** to see fewer posts like it. The ⋯ menu also lets you **report** a post or a comment.

**Sponsored** posts are Spotlights an organizer or place paid to show to more people. They are always marked *Sponsored*, and at most a small share of what you see is sponsored.

## Stories

**Stories** are photos and videos that disappear after 24 hours. They appear as a row of circles at the top of **Messages**, with people you have not caught up on first. Tap one to watch; tap or swipe to move on.

Replying to a Story or reacting to it sends a **private message** to its publisher, in Messages. It is not shown to anyone else.

To stop seeing someone's Stories without unfollowing them, choose **Mute Stories** from the Story's menu. You can unmute them the same way.

## Following

**Follow** an organizer or a place from their profile or page to see their posts under *Following* and their Stories in your row. Following is public: they see that you followed them, and follower counts are shown.

Following is separate from **Notify me**. *Notify me* is a private alert when they post a new event; you can use either, both or neither. See [Notifications](/help/customers/notifications).

## Who can post

Organizers with a published event, owners of a published place, verified organizers and Abonten can post Spotlights and Stories. Posting is not open to other accounts.

If you can post, **New Spotlight** appears in the app and on the website. Pick photos or a video, trim it, add a caption and optionally link one of your events or your place, then publish.

## Promoting a Spotlight

A publisher can pay to show a live Spotlight to more people. Choose a budget and see an **estimated** reach before paying. Abonten reviews every promotion before it runs.

- You are charged for what is delivered. Budget that was not used is shown on the promotion; contact support about it.
- A promotion that is rejected in review is refunded in full.
- Promotions are paid in cash only; Abonten Credit cannot be used for them.

## Blocking and reporting

Blocking someone hides their Spotlights, Stories and comments from you, and yours from them — see [Messaging](/help/customers/messaging). Report posts that break the rules from the ⋯ menu; see [Reporting a problem](/help/customers/reporting-a-problem).
