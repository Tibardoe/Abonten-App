# Reporting a problem

## What you can report

Events, places, event reviews, place reviews, user reviews, highlights, messages, conversations, users and organizers — each has a **Report** option in its menu.

## How to report

1. Choose **Report**.
2. Pick a reason: spam · fraud or scam · misleading · harassment · inappropriate · fake listing · safety · copyright · impersonation · other.
3. Describe what is wrong (up to 2,000 characters) and attach screenshots or files if useful.
4. Submit. You will see a confirmation.

You can have one open report per item; a second report on the same item is not accepted until the first is decided. Reports are limited to ten per hour per account. You cannot report your own account.

## What happens next

Reports go to Abonten's moderation team. Depending on the reason and what they find, they may leave the content up, **restrict** a listing (it cannot be featured), **hide** it while investigating, **remove** it, remove an organizer's or owner's reply to a review, or **suspend or ban** an account. Safety, fraud, harassment and exploitation reports are prioritised.

For privacy reasons we do not share the detailed outcome of a report with the reporter.

## If something was done to your content or account

Abonten does not have a formal appeals form yet. Contact support through the app or website; a staff member with the right permission can review the decision and reverse it if it was wrong. See [Restricted accounts](/help/account/restricted-accounts).

## Emergencies

If someone is in danger, contact the Ghana Police Service or emergency services first. Abonten reports are not monitored around the clock.

## Copyright

If content on Abonten uses your work without permission, report it with the **copyright** reason and describe the work and your rights to it.
