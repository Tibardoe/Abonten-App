# Sending a ticket to a friend

If you can't go, or you bought tickets for a group, you can send a ticket to someone else's Abonten account. They get their own ticket with its own code, so nobody has to wait outside for the person holding everyone's tickets.

> **Not available to everyone yet.** Sending tickets is being tried by Abonten staff. This page describes how it works once it opens.

## Sending

1. Open the ticket and tap **Send to a friend**.
2. Choose how to find them: by **username**, or by **phone number**.
   - With a username, you see their name and photo before you send, so you can check it's the right person.
   - With a phone number, we don't say whether the number has an account. If it doesn't, we text it a link, and they accept by signing up with that number.
3. Tap **Send ticket**.

The ticket stays yours until they accept it. Until then you can tap **Take it back**. If nobody accepts it within 48 hours, it stays with you.

You can send a ticket right up to the moment it's scanned, so if a friend is late you can send their ticket from inside the venue.

## Receiving

A ticket sent to you appears at the top of **My Tickets**. Tap **Accept** to take it, or **Decline** to leave it with the sender. When you accept, the ticket moves to your account with a **new code**. The old code, the sender's emailed PDF and any screenshot stop working.

## Rules

- Only an unused ticket can be sent. A ticket that has been scanned at the door can't be.
- A ticket can be passed on at most three times.
- Some organizers switch sending off for their events, for example when guests must be named in advance.
- You can't send a ticket to someone who has blocked you.
- Free events allow one ticket per person. A free ticket you sent still counts as yours, so you can't register again.

## Refunds

A refund always goes to **the person who paid**. If someone sent you a ticket and you can't go, send it back to them. They can cancel it and get their money back under the event's rules. You can't cancel a ticket someone else paid for.

## Buying a ticket from someone

Only accept tickets that arrive in **your own Abonten account**. A PDF, a screenshot or a forwarded email may have been sold to several people, and only the first one to the door gets in. Abonten never sees money paid between people, so it cannot refund it.

## Your ticket code

Your ticket's code is hidden until you tap **Show code**, so a photo of your ticket doesn't give it away. Keep it private: anyone who has it could try to use your ticket.
