# Paying small small

Some events let you pay for your tickets bit by bit instead of all at once. You lock today's price, your tickets are held for you, and you pay what you can, when you can, until the deadline. As soon as everything is paid, your tickets are issued and emailed to you, exactly as if you had paid in full.

> **Not available to everyone yet.** Paying small small is being tried by Abonten staff. This page describes how it works once it opens.

It is not a loan: nothing is added to the price, and you get your tickets only once they are fully paid.

## Starting

1. On the event, start buying tickets as usual and choose your tickets (up to four at a time).
2. Under **How do you want to pay?**, choose **Pay small small**. You only see it when the organizer offers it and the event is far enough away.
3. Read the terms shown: your first payment, the smallest later payment, the deadline, and what happens if you don't finish.
4. Tap **Start paying small small**. Your tickets are held for 30 minutes while you make your first payment.

## Paying

- **First payment:** at least 20% of the total (the price plus the service fee).
- **After that:** any amount of at least 10% of the total, whenever you like. Your last payment can be whatever is left.
- Pay with mobile money or a card, the same way as at checkout.
- **Deadline:** 48 hours before the event starts. The exact time is on your plan.

You will get a reminder 7 days and 2 days before the deadline if something is still left to pay.

Find your plans under **Balance** (in the app's Account tab, or your account menu on the website). Each plan shows what you have paid, what is left, the deadline and every payment you made.

## When it's fully paid

Your tickets are issued straight away. They appear with your other tickets and we email them to you. The organizer is paid for them like any other sale.

## If you don't finish, or you cancel

If the deadline passes before you have paid everything, or you cancel the plan yourself, or the organizer cancels the event:

- your held tickets go back on sale;
- what you paid comes back to the mobile money number or card you paid with, **minus the service-fee share of it**, which is not refunded (the same rule as every refund on Abonten). The plan shows you exactly how much before you cancel.

Refunds are sent automatically, each payment back to where it came from. They can take a few days to reach you, depending on your bank or mobile money network. If part of a refund is delayed, we tell you and our team follows it up.

## Your Balance

**Balance** shows two different things side by side, and never adds them together:

- **Saved towards tickets** — money you have paid on plans that are still running. It is yours: it goes to your tickets when a plan is finished, or comes back to you if a plan ends.
- **Abonten Credit** — credit you earned from rewards, if rewards are open to you. You can use it at checkout; it cannot be cashed out and some of it expires. See [Rewards and credit](/help/customers/rewards-and-credit).

Abonten has no wallet you can top up with money to spend later.

## Questions

- **Can I use a promo code?** Not on a plan. Promo codes work when you pay in full.
- **Can I change the tickets on a plan?** No. Cancel the plan and start a new one (the service-fee share of what you paid is not refunded).
- **The organizer turned Pay small small off.** Plans already running carry on as normal.

Something wrong with a plan? Message Abonten support: on the website, **Messages › Contact Abonten Support**; in the app, **Account › Help & support** (see [Messaging](/help/customers/messaging)). For how refunds work in general, see [Refunds and cancellations](/help/customers/refunds-and-cancellations).
