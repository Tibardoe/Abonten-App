# Your tickets

## Where they are

- **Web:** Manage › **My Tickets**, with *Active*, *Past*, *Cancelled* and *Refunds* tabs and a *To review / Reviewed* view for events you attended.
- **App:** the **Tickets** tab.

Each ticket shows the event, date, ticket type, a unique ticket code (starting `TKT-`) and a QR code.

## At the door

Show the QR code. The organizer either scans it with the Abonten app or finds you on their attendee list and checks you in. A ticket is valid **once**: after check-in it is marked *used* and cannot be used again. Do not share your QR code — whoever holds it can be checked in in your place.

## Receipts and PDFs

Open a ticket to download it as a PDF or share it. If your account has an email address, your tickets were also emailed when you bought them.

## Ticket statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Active | Valid for entry |
| Used | You were checked in |
| Cancelled | You cancelled it, or the organizer cancelled the event ("Cancelled by organizer") |
| Expired | The event has passed without check-in |

## Something is wrong

- **I paid but there is no ticket** — go to the checkout or your transaction and use **Retry**; it re-checks the payment and issues the ticket. If it still does not appear, contact support with the payment time and amount.
- **The organizer says my ticket is already used** — a ticket can only be checked in once. If you did not use it, someone with your QR code may have; contact the organizer and support.
- **The event details changed** — the organizer edited the event. Your ticket stays valid for the updated details. If the event is cancelled, see [Refunds and cancellations](/help/customers/refunds-and-cancellations).
