# Abonten Safety

Abonten Safety lets you name up to five people you trust and reach them fast when you need them: on a night out, at an event, or on the way home. Open it from **Account › Safety** in the app, or **Safety** in your account menu on the website.

> **Not available to everyone yet.** Abonten Safety is being tried by Abonten staff. This page describes how it works once it opens.

> **Abonten Safety is not an emergency service.** Abonten does not contact the police, an ambulance or the fire service. If you are in danger, call **112** first.

## Before you start

1. Add your name to your profile and verify your phone number. Your contacts see both, so they know the alert is really from you.
2. Add your trusted contacts: a name, their phone number, how you know them and the language they read. Each one gets a text asking whether they agree (you can send it again if they missed it). They can accept, say no, or stop all Abonten Safety texts to their number.
3. The app keeps your contacts on your phone too, so SOS works even when you have no internet.

Texts go to numbers in Ghana. A contact with a number elsewhere can still be reached by email (add one), in the Abonten app if they accepted while signed in, or by a text you send from your own phone.

## SOS

Hold the **SOS** button for a second and a half. Letting go early sends nothing, so a pocket press does no harm.

- Every trusted contact gets a text straight away with a link to a page that shows **where you are**, how recently your position was updated, the event you are at if you chose one, a button to call you, and the emergency number.
- Your screen shows, for each contact, whether the text went, whether they opened the page and what they answered: **on my way**, **calling you**, or **can't help right now**.
- You can add a note for your contacts at any time ("near the main stage with Esi").
- If Abonten cannot be reached, your phone's own Messages app opens with your contacts and your location already filled in: just press send. Abonten also sends the alert as soon as your phone is back online.

When you are safe, tap **I'm safe**. Everyone who was alerted is told, and they can no longer see where you are. If you pressed SOS by mistake, tap **That was a mistake** instead.

## Check in on me

Choose a time, for example when you expect to be home. Nobody is told anything unless you miss it.

- Abonten reminds you five minutes before, and at the time.
- Tap **I'm safe** before the time, or add 15, 30 or 60 minutes if you need longer.
- If you have not answered five minutes after the time, your trusted contacts get an alert with your last known location, as with SOS.

## Share my location

Share your live location with the contacts you choose for up to 12 hours, for example while you travel home. They get a text with a link to follow you. It ends when the time is up or when you tap **Stop sharing**. If something goes wrong on the way, hold SOS: all your trusted contacts are alerted, and the people already following you keep the same page.

## If someone named you as a trusted contact

- You get a text asking whether you agree. You can accept, say no, or stop all Abonten Safety texts to your number. Accepting while signed in to Abonten also brings alerts to your app.
- If they need you, you get a text with a link. The page shows where they are while their alert is open. Answer from the page so they know you saw it.
- You stop seeing where they are as soon as they say they are safe. An alert nobody closed stays visible for one more day after it runs out.
- You can stop being someone's contact at any time from the link in your first text.

## Things to know

- **Your position is sent while the app is open.** If your phone is locked or the app is closed, your contacts see your last position and how old it is. Keep the app open during an alert if you can.
- **Texts can be late or fail**, and a contact may not see the alert in time. Choose contacts who keep their phone close, and call 112 if you are in danger.
- **Your position is deleted 7 days after** an SOS, check-in or share ends, and its history (when it ran, who was told and what they answered) after 180 days. Deleting your account deletes your contacts and all of this at once.
- Abonten Safety does not tell event organizers about your alert.

Questions or a problem: contact Abonten Support from **Messages** in the app or on the website.
