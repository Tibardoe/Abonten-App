# Reviews and highlights

## Reviewing an event

You can review an event when **all** of these are true:

- you are signed in and you are not the organizer;
- the event (or its last date) has ended and was not cancelled;
- you hold a ticket that was **checked in** at the event;
- you have not already reviewed it.

Events waiting for your review appear under **My Tickets › To review** (web) or in the Tickets tab (app). A review has a star rating, an optional title (up to 150 characters), a comment (up to 500 characters) and optional photos. You can edit or delete your own review later.

## Reviewing a place

Anyone signed in can review a place from its page; the same rating, title, comment and photo rules apply. One review per place per person — edit it rather than writing another.

## Replies

The organizer or owner can post **one public response** to your review and may edit or remove it. They cannot delete your review. If a response is abusive, report it.

## Where reviews appear

Reviews show on the event or place page and under the reviewer's public profile (*Reviews* tab). Reviews that break the rules may be hidden or removed by Abonten; the author still sees their own hidden review.

## Drafts

If you close a review before posting it, it is saved as a draft and offered the next time you open the review form.

## Highlights

Highlights are short photo or video stories on your profile, like a story ring. Post one from your profile page (web) or **Account › your profile › add highlight** (app): pick photos or a video, crop or trim, and upload — a progress bar shows the upload. You can delete a whole highlight or a single slide later. Viewers can report a highlight; there are no comments or reactions on highlights.

## Rules

Reviews must be your own honest experience. Fake, paid-for, coordinated or self-reviews, and reviews containing harassment, hate or personal data about other people, are removed and may lead to account action. See the Terms and Conditions.
