# Deleting your account

## Before you delete

- **Tickets:** active tickets stay valid records but you will no longer be able to open them in Abonten. Download any you still need.
- **Organizers:** events you own are deleted with your account. Cancel upcoming events first so attendees are refunded and notified, and request any available payout — an available balance is not paid out automatically.
- **Place owners:** places you own are deleted with your account. If someone else should take the listing over, have them claim it first.
- **Abonten Credit:** any balance is forfeited and pending rewards are cancelled.

## How to delete

**Web:** Settings › Security › **Delete account**. **App:** Settings › Security › **Delete account**, then confirm twice.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. You are signed out everywhere.

## What is deleted

Your sign-in, profile, phone and email, events and places you own, favourites, highlights, saved payout accounts and notification settings.

## What is kept

For financial accountability, security and legal reasons Abonten keeps: payment transactions and tickets that were already issued, organizer ledger and refund records, reports you filed (with your identity removed), moderation and administrative logs, rewards ledger history, and — if you were on an Abonten field team or gave onboarding consent — that programme's audit records. Details are in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## Uninstalling the app

Removing the app from your phone does not delete your account; it only removes the app's local data and stops push notifications for that device.
