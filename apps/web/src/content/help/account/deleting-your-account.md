# Deleting your account

## Before you delete

- **Tickets:** tickets you bought stay valid records, but you will no longer be able to open them in Abonten. Download any you still need.
- **Organizers:** you cannot delete your account while an upcoming event of yours still has attendees, while a payout to you is being processed, or while you have earnings waiting to be paid out. Cancel those events first (attendees are refunded and told) and request any available payout; the delete screen tells you which step is still open.
- **Spotlight promotions:** you cannot delete your account while a Spotlight promotion you paid for is running or waiting for review, or while its payment is going through. Cancel it first.
- **Place owners:** places you own stay listed but become unclaimed, so the next owner can claim them. If someone else should take a listing over, have them claim it first. Until then nobody can message the place or send it a booking request.
- **Abonten Credit:** any balance is forfeited and pending rewards are cancelled.

## How to delete

**Web:** Settings › Security › **Delete account**. **App:** Settings › Security › **Delete account**, then confirm twice.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. You are signed out everywhere. The same email address or phone number can be used to create a new account later; nothing from the old one carries over.

## What is deleted

Your sign-in, name, username, photo, bio, phone and email, favourites, reminders, highlights, notifications and notification settings, drafts, and pending place claims. Saved cards and mobile-money numbers are removed (the details that could charge them are erased). Payout account details are erased. Draft events are deleted; upcoming events with nobody attending are cancelled; your other events are archived and no longer appear in discovery. Your Spotlight posts and Stories are taken down and their photos and videos removed (one that someone reported is kept a little longer so the report can be reviewed), and you stop following the accounts you followed.

## What is kept

For financial accountability, security and legal reasons Abonten keeps, against a profile shown as a former Abonten member: payment transactions and tickets that were already issued, organizer ledger and refund records, reviews and messages you wrote, reports you filed, moderation and administrative logs, rewards ledger history, and — if you were on an Abonten field team or gave onboarding consent — that programme's audit records. Details are in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## Uninstalling the app

Removing the app from your phone does not delete your account; it only removes the app's local data and stops push notifications for that device.
