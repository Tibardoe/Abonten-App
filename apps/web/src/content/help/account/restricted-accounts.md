# Restricted accounts

If you sign in and see **"Your account is restricted"** (web) or **"Your account has been restricted"** (app), an Abonten administrator has suspended or banned your account. While restricted you cannot use the service; the only actions available are signing out and contacting support.

## Why it happens

Accounts are restricted for breaches of the [Terms and Conditions](/legal/terms) — for example fraud or scams, fake listings, harassment, abuse of the rewards programme, repeated false reports, or a payment dispute raised in bad faith — or as a precaution when we suspect an account has been taken over.

## Suspension vs ban

- **Suspended** is temporary in intent: access is paused while something is investigated or corrected.
- **Banned** is a final decision. Bans require a senior administrator to re-authenticate before they are applied.

Either way, every device is signed out at the moment the action is taken, and Abonten records who took the action and why.

## Your tickets, events and money

Restriction does not delete anything. Tickets already issued remain records; refunds already requested continue; organizer earnings stay in the ledger, though payouts connected to the conduct in question may be withheld.

## How to contact us

Abonten does not have a formal appeals form yet. Email **support@abontenhub.com** from the email address on your account (or, if you can still reach it, use the **in-app support conversation**). Explain what happened; an administrator with the right permission can review and, where appropriate, restore your account.

## Restored accounts

If an administrator restores your account, you can sign in again immediately.
