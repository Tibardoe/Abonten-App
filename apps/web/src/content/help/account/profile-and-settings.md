# Your profile and settings

## Your public profile

Your profile (web: `/user/<username>`; app: Account › your name) shows your avatar, name, username, bio and website, and tabs for the events you have posted, the places you own, your reviews, and — visible only to you — your favourites and bookings.

## Edit your profile

**Settings › Edit profile** (web and app): name, username, bio, website, and avatar. Avatars are cropped in the app or browser and uploaded straight to our image host; a progress bar shows the upload. Usernames must be unique. A "complete your profile" reminder appears until you have set a name and username.

## Phone number and email

**Settings › Security**:

- **Phone** — add or change your number. We text a code to the **new** number; enter it to confirm. The code expires in 5 minutes.
- **Email** — add or change your address. We email a 6-digit code to the **new** address; enter it to confirm. A phone-only account that adds a verified email can then also sign in with Google or an email code.

Both flows work without signing you out.

## Language

**Settings › Language**: English, French, Spanish, German, Portuguese, Akan. The choice is saved on this device (a cookie on the web).

## Appearance

**Settings › Switch appearance**: light, dark or follow the system.

## Wallet and payout accounts

Saved payment methods live in **Wallet**. Organizer payout accounts live in **Finances › Payout accounts** — see [Finance, payouts and settlement](/help/organizers/finance-payouts-and-settlement).

## Delete your account

**Settings › Security › Delete account**. See [Deleting your account](/help/account/deleting-your-account) for exactly what is removed and what is kept.
