# Claiming and verifying a place

## When to claim

A place may already be on Abonten — added by another user, or by an Abonten field team with a previous owner's consent — while you, the actual owner, have no control over it. **Claiming** asks Abonten to transfer the listing to your account.

If the place is **not** on Abonten yet, don't claim — [add it yourself](/help/place-owners/managing-your-place).

## Submit a claim

1. Sign in and open the place page.
2. Choose **Claim this place** (it appears only when you are not already the owner).
3. Add an optional note, a contact phone and email, and any supporting documents (for example a business registration, utility bill or signage photo).
4. Submit. You can have one pending claim per place.

## Review

Abonten staff review claims in the admin console. They may message you for more information. When a decision is made you are notified in the app:

- **Approved** — the listing moves to your account and is marked **claimed and verified**. You can now edit everything.
- **Rejected** — the listing stays as it was. You can submit a new claim with better evidence.

Supporting documents are stored privately and **deleted 30 days after the decision**.

## What "verified" means

A verified badge means Abonten confirmed the person managing the listing is its owner — either through an approved claim or, for listings created by an Abonten field team, through the owner's own one-time code. Owners cannot set the verified badge themselves.

## Disputes

If someone else claims a place you own, Abonten reviews both sides' evidence. Filing a claim for a business you do not own breaks the Terms and can lead to account restriction.

## Field-team listings

If an Abonten team member listed your business with your consent, the listing was created under **your** account from the start — you do not need to claim it. Sign in with the phone number that received the consent code to find it under Account › Places (app) or Manage › Places (web).
