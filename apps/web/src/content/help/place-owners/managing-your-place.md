# Adding and managing your place

## Add a place

- **Web:** side menu › **Create** › Place. **App:** Account › Organizer › Places › **New place**.

Your progress is saved as a draft until you publish. Fill in:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Name, category, description | Category drives where the place appears in Explore. |
| Location | Search the address or drop a pin. The pin is what "near you" and directions use — be precise. |
| Contact | Phone, WhatsApp number, website. Shown as buttons on your page. |
| Opening hours | Per day, including closed days and split hours. Your page shows "open now" / "closed" from these. |
| Services | Things people can book or ask about (e.g. table reservation, private room), each with an optional price note. |
| Cover and gallery photos | Uploaded straight to our image host. |

Check Abonten first for an existing listing of your business — if one exists, [claim it](/help/place-owners/claiming-and-verifying-a-place) rather than creating a duplicate.

## Publish

Publishing makes the place visible in Explore, search and the map, and on your public profile under *Places*.

## Manage your listing

Open it under Manage › Places (web) or Organizer › Places (app):

- **Edit** details, contact and location.
- **Photos:** add, remove, reorder, and set the cover.
- **Opening hours** and **services**.
- **Status:** mark the place **temporarily closed** or **permanently closed**; the badge shows on your page and it is excluded from "open now".
- **Bookings** and **Reviews** — see [Bookings, reviews and messaging](/help/place-owners/bookings-reviews-and-messaging).
- **Promote** — see [Promoting your place](/help/place-owners/promoting-your-place).
- **Visit QR** — where the rewards programme is live, a rotating QR code visitors can scan at your place.

## Insights

The place's **Insights** show page views and clicks on your contact buttons (call, WhatsApp, website, directions) over time.

## Events at your place

Events whose venue matches your place appear on your page under *Upcoming events*.

## Keep it accurate

Wrong hours or locations are the most common complaints. Misleading listings can be reported and restricted; permanently closed businesses should be marked as such rather than deleted, so people are not misled by search results elsewhere.
