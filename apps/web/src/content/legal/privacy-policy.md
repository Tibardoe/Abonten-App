# Privacy Policy

> **Draft for review.** This policy describes what Abonten Hub actually does with personal information today. It has been prepared with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) in mind but has not yet been reviewed by legal counsel, and some organisational details (the data controller's registered identity, registration with the Data Protection Commission, and contact addresses) are still to be confirmed.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

Abonten Hub ("Abonten", "we") operates the abontenhub.com website and the Abonten mobile app and decides how personal information collected through them is used. The legal entity acting as data controller, its registered address and its Data Protection Commission registration details will be stated here once confirmed.

Organizers and place owners who receive attendee or customer information through Abonten (section 6) are responsible for how they use it.

## 3. How we use it

We use personal information to:

1. **Provide the service** — create your account, sign you in, show listings, issue tickets, deliver messages and notifications, process bookings and claims.
2. **Take and return payments** — through Paystack, including refunds when you or an organizer cancel, and payouts to organizers.
3. **Keep the service safe** — verify phone numbers, limit how often codes and requests can be sent, detect duplicate accounts and rewards abuse, investigate reports, and enforce our Terms.
4. **Run the rewards programme** — attribute referrals, decide and pay rewards, and screen them for abuse.
5. **Communicate with you** — ticket confirmations, event cancellation notices, rewards updates (which you can turn off), support replies, and important notices about your account or these policies.
6. **Understand and improve the service** — aggregated statistics such as ticket sales per event for organizers, place-page views and clicks for owners, and error and performance monitoring for our engineers.
7. **Meet legal obligations** — keep financial records, respond to lawful requests, and handle disputes.

**Legal grounds.** Under the Data Protection Act, 2012, we rely on your consent (for example when you sign up, allow location or notifications, or give onboarding consent by code), the performance of our contract with you (providing the service you asked for), our legitimate interests in running a safe and reliable service, and compliance with law. Which ground applies to each purpose is subject to legal review.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for third-party advertising.

## 5. Sharing with other users

Some information is visible to other users by design:

- **Public profile**: your username, name, avatar, bio, website, the events and places you have published, your public highlights and your reviews.
- **Organizers** see the name and contact details of people holding tickets to their events, so they can run the event and check people in.
- **Place owners** see the details in booking requests sent to them, and the name and review of anyone who reviews their place.
- **Message recipients** see what you send them and when you were last active in the conversation.
- **People you invite** see your first name or username on the invite page.

Favourites and bookings are private to you.

## 6. Organizers and place owners as recipients

When an organizer or owner receives your information through Abonten, they may use it only for the event or booking it relates to. Our Terms prohibit them from selling it or using it for unrelated marketing. If you believe an organizer or owner has misused your information, report them in the app.

## 7. Cookies and device storage

The website uses a small number of first-party cookies for signing in, remembering your language and country, attributing invites and referrals, and detecting rewards abuse. The app stores equivalent information securely on your device. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. The [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies) lists every cookie and storage key.

## 8. Notifications and marketing

- **In-app and push notifications** are sent for activity on your account (tickets, messages, bookings, reviews, rewards, event changes). You can turn off push notifications in your device settings. Abonten does not yet offer per-type notification controls in the app.
- **Emails** are sent for ticket confirmations, event cancellations and sign-in codes. **Rewards emails** ("your credit is ready") can be turned off on the Rewards page or with the unsubscribe link in every such email.
- Abonten does not send marketing newsletters or share your contact details with advertisers. If we introduce marketing communications we will ask for your consent first.

## 9. How long we keep it

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Account, profile, listings, favourites, highlights | Until you delete your account |
| Tickets, transactions, refunds, organizer earnings and payouts | Retained after account deletion as financial records; specific period to be confirmed |
| Pending checkouts | Expire after 30 minutes if unpaid; expired rows are cleaned up |
| Drafts (events, places, reviews) | Until published or deleted; expired drafts are removed automatically |
| Messages and attachments | Until deleted by you (where supported) or by moderation; no automatic expiry today |
| Reports and moderation records | Retained; your identity is removed from reports you filed when you delete your account |
| Place-claim supporting documents | Deleted 30 days after the claim is approved or rejected |
| Referral click records | 90 days |
| Rewards ledger and reward decisions | Retained after account deletion (the balance itself is forfeited) |
| Field-programme evidence photos | Intended retention 365 days; automatic deletion is not yet in place |
| Rate-limit and one-time-code records | Rate-limit records 1 day; code request logs are retained (period to be confirmed) |
| Push tokens | Removed when you sign out of a device or the device stops accepting notifications |
| Error and performance monitoring | Sentry retains events per its plan; our own error records have no retention period yet (to be confirmed) |
| Administrative audit log | Retained permanently; it cannot be edited or deleted |

Where a period says "to be confirmed", no automatic deletion exists yet and a retention decision is pending. We will update this table when those decisions are made.

## 10. Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself from Settings › Security on the web or in the app. Deletion is immediate and irreversible:

- **Deleted**: your login, profile, phone and email, events and places you own, favourites, highlights, saved payout accounts, notification preferences, and rewards you were still waiting for.
- **Kept**: payment transactions and tickets already issued, ledger and refund records, reports you filed (with your identity removed), moderation and administrative logs, rewards ledger history, and — if you were a field-team member or gave onboarding consent — the programme's audit records. These are kept for financial accountability, security and legal reasons.
- **Forfeited**: any Abonten Credit balance.

Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account.

## 11. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2012 you have the right to:

- **access** the personal information we hold about you;
- **correct** information that is inaccurate — most of your details can be edited directly in Settings;
- **delete** your account (section 10);
- **object** to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing (we send none);
- **withdraw consent** where processing is based on it, for example by revoking location or notification permission on your device, or turning off rewards emails;
- **complain** to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana if you believe we have handled your information unlawfully.

To exercise a right, contact us through the in-app support conversation (Messages › "Contact Abonten Support" on the web, or Account › Help & support in the app). We will confirm your identity using the account you are signed into and respond within the period required by law. Abonten does not yet offer a self-service data export; access requests are handled manually.

## 12. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit, an authentication system with one-time codes instead of passwords, row-level database access rules that limit each account to its own data, server-side checks on every action, restricted staff access with an audit trail, and payment processing delegated to Paystack so that card data never touches our systems. Our security overview is at [abontenhub.com/legal/security](/legal/security). No system is perfectly secure; if you suspect a problem with your account, contact support at once.

## 13. Children

Abonten is not directed at children, but does not currently verify age. A minimum age and any parental-consent process are pending decisions and will be published here. If you believe a child has created an account, contact support and we will remove it.
