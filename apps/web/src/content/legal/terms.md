## 5. Tickets and free registrations

**Buying tickets.** Ticket prices are set by the organizer. At checkout you see the ticket price, Abonten's service fee (section 6) and the total before you pay. When you start a checkout, the tickets you selected are held for you for 30 minutes; if you do not pay within that time the hold is released and you must start again. You can buy at most 50 tickets of one type and 100 tickets in a single order.

**Free events.** For free events you register instead of paying. Registrations follow the same rules on cancellation and attendance.

**One order per event.** You cannot start a second checkout for an event while you already have a pending checkout or a ticket for it.

**Your ticket.** Each ticket has a unique code and QR code, shown in My Tickets on the web and in the Tickets tab of the app, and sent to your email address if you have one on your account. A ticket is valid once; it is marked as used when the organizer checks you in. Do not share your QR code — anyone holding it can be checked in in your place.

**Promo codes.** Organizers may issue promo codes that reduce the ticket price. A code applies only to the event it was created for, within any limits the organizer set. Codes cannot be exchanged for money.

**Changes to an event.** Organizers may edit an event's details. If an organizer cancels an event, every ticket for it is cancelled and paid tickets are refunded as described in section 7. Abonten will notify you in the app and, for paid tickets, by email.

## 6. Prices, fees and payment

**Currency.** Prices are shown and charged in Ghana cedis (GHS) unless the listing states otherwise.

**Service fee.** Abonten charges the customer a service fee on paid tickets, calculated as a percentage of the ticket price and shown as a separate "Service fee" line at checkout before you pay. The organizer receives the full ticket price they set; the service fee is Abonten's revenue.

**Payment processing.** Payments are processed by Paystack. You can pay by card or mobile money, and you can save a payment method for later. When you save a card, Paystack verifies it with a small charge (GHS 1.00) that is refunded immediately; Abonten stores only a reference from Paystack plus the last four digits, brand and expiry to display to you — never your full card number or PIN. Saved mobile-money wallets are stored as display details only.

**Failed or incomplete payments.** If a payment fails or you close the payment window, no ticket is issued and the checkout hold is released when it expires. If a payment succeeds but your ticket does not appear, the app offers a "retry" that re-checks the payment and issues the ticket; you can also contact support. You will not be charged twice for the same checkout.

**Paying with Abonten Credit.** Where the Abonten Rewards programme is available to you (section 12), you may be able to pay part of a ticket or a promotion with Abonten Credit; the credit portion is shown separately from the cash portion at checkout.

## 7. Cancellations and refunds

**Cancelling your own ticket.** You can cancel a ticket from My Tickets / the Tickets tab while the ticket is still active. Cancelling a ticket releases it for someone else to buy.

**Refunds when you cancel.** When you cancel a paid ticket, Abonten requests a refund of the **ticket price** for that ticket from Paystack. **The service fee is not refunded.** The refund goes back to the payment method you paid with. A refund is *requested* at the moment you cancel and is *completed* when Paystack confirms it; your transaction history shows "refund pending" in between. Refund timing after Paystack confirms depends on your bank or mobile-money provider.

**Refunds when the organizer cancels.** If the organizer cancels the event, Abonten requests a refund of the ticket price for every paid ticket automatically, on the same terms as above (the service fee is not refunded). If a refund request fails, a "retry refund" option appears on your ticket and support can re-request it.

**Refunds in other cases.** Abonten does not run a general "change of mind" refund scheme beyond ticket cancellation. Disputes about an event's quality, timing or content are between you and the organizer; you can contact the organizer through in-app messaging, and you can report the event to Abonten (section 14). Abonten may, at its discretion, issue a refund or a goodwill credit where our own systems have failed.

**Chargebacks.** If you raise a dispute with your bank instead of contacting us, we may pause your account while it is investigated, and the organizer's payout for that event may be held.

## 8. Organizers

If you create an event you are an organizer, and the following applies in addition to the rest of these terms.

**Your listing.** You are responsible for the accuracy of everything in your event listing — title, description, dates, venue, ticket types, prices, capacity and images — and for holding any permits, licences or venue permissions the event needs. You must not list events you do not have the right to sell tickets for.

**Ticket types and pricing.** You set ticket types, prices and quantities. Abonten shows the customer your price plus Abonten's service fee. You may create promo codes for your event and, where the feature is available, offer a promoter commission (a percentage of the ticket price you choose, between 1% and 30%) to people who bring buyers; that commission is charged to you when the sale settles.

**Your earnings.** For each paid ticket you receive **100% of the ticket price you set**, recorded as a pending earning at the time of sale. Earnings become available for payout **48 hours after your event ends** (after its last date, for multi-date events). Refunds reverse the corresponding earning. Your Finances page shows pending and available balances and every ledger entry.

**Payouts.** You request a payout of your available balance to a payout account (bank or mobile money) that you add in Finances. Payouts are processed by Abonten's finance team and paid by bank or mobile-money transfer; a payout may be held for review, for example when a large share of an event's sales was paid with Abonten Credit, or when a dispute is open. Abonten does not charge a payout fee at this time.

**Cancelling your event.** You may cancel a draft or published event. Cancelling is final: every ticket is cancelled, every paid ticket is refunded to the buyer (ticket price only), all attendees are notified, and the related earnings are reversed. The confirmation screen shows the number of paid tickets, free registrations and attendees affected before you confirm.

**Check-in.** You can check attendees in from the attendee list (web and app) or by scanning their QR codes with the app's camera. A ticket can be checked in once.

**Attendee data.** You can see the names and contact details of people who hold tickets to your events so that you can run the event. You may use that information only for the event in question, must keep it secure, and must not sell it, share it, or use it for unrelated marketing. Abonten's [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) explains what attendees are told.

**Reviews of your event.** Attendees who were checked in can review your event after it ends. You may post one public response to each review and may edit or remove your response. You cannot remove a review; you can report one that breaks section 13.

## 9. Places and place owners

**Listings.** A place (a venue, restaurant, bar, shop or other business) can be listed on Abonten by its owner or added by an Abonten field team with the owner's consent (section 11). The person who creates a place listing is its owner on Abonten unless ownership is transferred through a claim.

**Claiming a place.** If a place you own is listed but you do not control the listing, you can submit a claim from the place page with an optional note, contact details and supporting documents. Abonten staff review claims; if approved, the listing is transferred to you and marked verified. Supporting documents are deleted 30 days after a claim is decided. Submitting a claim for a place you do not own is prohibited.

**Managing a listing.** Owners can edit the listing, opening hours, services, photos, temporary status (for example "closed today"), and can promote the place (section 10). You are responsible for keeping the listing accurate.

**Bookings.** Customers can send a booking **request** to a place, with an optional service, time, party size and note. A request is not a confirmed reservation until the owner accepts it in the app; owners can accept or decline, and customers can cancel their request. Abonten does not take payment for bookings and is not a party to any arrangement between you and the customer.

**Reviews and replies.** Customers can review a place; owners may post one public response per review. Owners cannot remove reviews but may report them.

**Place visits.** Where the Abonten Rewards programme is running, an owner may display a rotating QR code that visitors scan to record a visit. A visit is recorded only within roughly 150 metres of the place and at most once per person per day.
