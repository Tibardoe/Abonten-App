# Claim assistance

When the duplicate search shows the business is **already on Abonten** (same phone, or a strong name match nearby), do not create another listing. Help the real owner **claim** the existing one.

## Steps

1. From the match, choose **Help the owner claim this listing**.
2. Owner consent by OTP or link — exactly as for an onboarding. The owner must be the business's owner, not you or any team member.
3. Submit. A **claim request** is filed on the listing with the **owner as the claimant** (never you). Abonten staff review claims in the console; the owner may be asked for documents.

Refused automatically: claiming a listing you (or a colleague) own; a listing the owner already holds; a second assistance for the same owner/listing (the first one stands).

## Payment

Claim assistance pays on the **claim_approved** release policy: after the lead's verification and holding period, the sweep waits for staff to decide the claim. **Approved** → your commission is approved; **rejected** → nothing. The claim is not checked for photos or hours — only that it was filed for the right owner and decided.

## Disputes

If someone else later tries to claim a listing you onboarded, the system flags your onboarding for an admin to look at (`fieldops_flag_on_claim`). That is normal; answer your lead's questions and keep your evidence.
