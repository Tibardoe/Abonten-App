# Working a day

## Before you start

Your lead assigns you to a **territory** (a town or area: a centre with a radius, or a drawn boundary) for a day or a range of days. You get a notification. You can hold one open assignment per territory at a time; a reassignment is a cancellation (with a reason you can see) plus a new assignment.

## Start

`/field` → **Start** on today's assignment.

- **Offline members:** allow location. Your position, its accuracy and the distance to the territory centre are recorded and shown to your lead. You are **never refused** for being far away — the distance is information, not a gate — but a pattern of check-ins far from the territory will be questioned.
- **Online members:** Start records the time only.
- Starting is possible only while the campaign is **active** (a paused campaign blocks starting; work already started can be completed).

## Prospects

Open the territory → **Add a business** for every business or organizer you speak to, whether or not they say yes: name, contact person, phone or WhatsApp, and a note. If the duplicate search shows the business is already on Abonten, the prospect links to that listing ("already on Abonten") and you can offer [claim assistance](claim-assistance.md) instead.

Update the prospect as it progresses: **identified → contacted → interested / declined → converted** (converted happens automatically when an onboarding is submitted). Each contact attempt you log is kept.

Prospects need an open assignment in that territory. Phone numbers you enter are visible to you and your lead **masked**; only an admin with the PII permission sees them in full.

## Onboarding

From the territory page or a prospect: **Onboard this business** → the [wizard](onboarding-places.md). You may submit up to the daily cap (8 by default) per day.

## Mark completed

At the end of the day: **Complete** on the assignment. Your lead's coverage board shows covered / completed / uncovered territories.

## What is recorded

Assignment times and positions; prospects with contact attempts; onboardings with the position at submission, its distance to the pin, whether it was inside the territory, the duplicate-search results, and every status change with who made it. This is what protects you when a payment is questioned — and what makes fraud visible.

## Common problems

- **"Field work" link missing** — programme or worker UI switched off, or your membership is not active (invited numbers bind when you sign in with that phone; ask your lead to check the team page).
- **Cannot start** — campaign paused, assignment not for today, or you already started it.
- **Location denied** — allow it in the browser's site settings, reload, Start again.
