# Conduct, privacy and security for field teams

## How to treat people

- Introduce yourself as working **with** Abonten on a listing programme; say plainly that listing is free, that the owner will control the listing from their own account, and that a code will be sent to their phone to confirm.
- Never pressure anyone. "No" ends the conversation; log the prospect as declined.
- Never promise sales, rankings, featured placement or prices. Featuring is a paid product the owner buys themselves.
- Never take payment from a business or organizer for anything.

## Privacy duties

- You collect the **minimum**: business details, the owner's name and phone, and the evidence photos the programme requires.
- Owner phone numbers are **masked** for you and your lead in the app; write them nowhere else — not in a notebook, WhatsApp group or spreadsheet.
- Photos of premises only; no customers' faces without their agreement; no children.
- Consent codes are for the owner's eyes only. Never read one aloud from someone else's phone, never enter one you did not watch the owner receive.
- Evidence photos are private and stay in Abonten's private storage; do not keep copies.
- If a business owner asks to remove their listing or their data, tell them they can edit or ask Abonten support, and tell your lead.

## Security duties

- Your Abonten account is your work identity: keep your phone locked; sign out of shared devices; report a lost phone to your lead the same day so your membership can be suspended.
- Only use `/field` on abontenhub.com. Nobody from Abonten will ever ask you for a one-time code.
- Do not share your account or let someone else work under your name — every action is recorded under the account that did it.

## Prohibited (leads to removal and, where relevant, to Abonten account action)

Fabricating a business, event or organizer · entering a consent code yourself or from a colleague's phone · onboarding a business you or a team member owns · reusing photos or evidence across submissions · GPS spoofing · inflating content figures · claiming a listing for yourself · taking money from owners · sharing or misusing owners' data · harassing or misleading anyone.

## What the system records (so you know)

Every start/complete with position, every prospect and contact log, every onboarding step and status change with who did it, every commission and payment. These records are kept after you leave the team and after account deletion, for accountability.

## Escalation

1. Safety — remove yourself from the situation; tell your lead; contact emergency services if needed.
2. Owner disputes consent or a listing's accuracy — tell your lead the same day; do not argue with the owner about codes.
3. Suspected fraud by a colleague — tell your lead or, if it concerns the lead, the programme manager through Abonten support (say "field programme").
4. Anything about your pay — earnings page first, then your lead, then the programme manager.

Acknowledging this handbook is required before your first assignment (decision P4: how acknowledgement is recorded).
