# Team lead guide

## Set up the towns

`/field/lead/territories` → **Add territory**: name, centre (use "Find on the map" or type coordinates) and radius, or paste a boundary. Mark a territory **completed** when it is fully worked; reopen if needed. Territories must be inside the campaign's region.

## Build the team

`/field/lead/team` → **Invite by phone**: the member joins when they sign in with that number (a verified phone is required by default). Set the role: offline member, online member or content creator. You cannot appoint another lead or change your own row. **Suspend** (temporarily stops work; open assignments cancel), **Reactivate**, **Remove**.

## Plan days

`/field/lead/assignments` → pick the day → assign **member × territory × dates**. Rules enforced: member active and of the right mode (offline/online), territory active and in the region, one open assignment per member per territory, no past dates. **Cancel** with a reason the member sees; reassign = cancel + new. Members get a notification for each change.

## Announce

`/field/lead/announce` → message every active member (in-app + push if the programme's push switch is on).

## Review submissions

`/field/lead/review` lists **submitted** first. Open one: the business, the owner's masked phone, photos, **evidence** (signed links), position and distance, duplicate matches, and the **eligibility checklist** (pending checks show as pending, not as failures). Decide:

- **Verify** — starts the holding period and records the pending commission at the live rule (campaign override if one exists).
- **Needs changes** — note required; the member fixes and resubmits.
- **Reject** — note required; no commission.

You cannot review your own submission. Reviews left open past a completed campaign's grace period (14 days) are closed by housekeeping.

## Review content

`/field/lead/content` — write briefs; approve or reject the creator's submissions (rejection needs a reason). Figures are self-reported; do not treat them as evidence of reach.

## Performance

`/field/lead/performance` — your campaign's figures: coverage (from real assignments), the prospect funnel, onboardings by status, per-member totals including the median hours work waits for your review. Same data as the admin's Figures page, scoped to your campaign.

## Escalate to the programme manager when

A member's check-ins are consistently far from their territory; evidence looks reused; an owner says they never consented; two members onboard the same business; anything involving money you cannot explain from the earnings page.
