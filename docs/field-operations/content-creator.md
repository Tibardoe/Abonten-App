# Content creator guide

## Briefs

Your lead writes **briefs** at `/field/lead/content`: what the campaign wants made (topic, platform, deadline, notes). Everyone on the campaign can read them; only the active content creator can submit against them.

## Submitting

`/field/content` → **Submit**: platform (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, WhatsApp…), the **post URL**, a caption, and your **self-reported** figures (views, likes, shares). Rules:

- One submission per post URL, programme-wide (the same link cannot be submitted twice by anyone).
- The URL must be a real `http(s)` link.
- Figures are stored and shown as **self-reported** in every screen and are **never used to decide payment**. Do not inflate them; they are for the lead's and Abonten's information only.

## Review and payment

The lead approves or rejects (a rejection needs a reason). Approval snapshots the live `content_deliverable` rule, starts a **holding period** and records a **pending** commission. The 15-minute sweep confirms it after the holding period — nothing about a post on another platform can be checked automatically, so the holding period is really a window in which a human can retract. A creator who leaves the team during the holding period is not paid for pending items.

## Stipend

The content creator and the team lead receive a **monthly stipend** at the live stipend rules. An admin runs it for the campaign each month (Admin › Field Ops › Content › Run monthly stipends); it appears as an approved commission on your earnings page and is paid in the next weekly batch. Idempotent — one per member per month.

## Standards

Only content about businesses and events that are listed with consent; no personal data of owners or customers beyond what is public on Abonten; no claims about prices or offers the business did not confirm; respect platform rules. The [evidence and content standards](evidence-photo-and-content-standards.md) apply.
