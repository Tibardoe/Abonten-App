# Listing, photo, evidence and content standards

## The eligibility checklist

After the lead verifies, the automatic sweep (`fieldops_evaluate_onboarding`) applies these checks. **Hard** failures reject; **soft** failures flag for an admin; a random **spot check** (5% by default) also flags.

| Check | Hard / soft | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| Listing exists, is published, is not hidden/removed by moderation | hard | Tell the owner not to unpublish before the holding period ends |
| Owner unchanged and is the verified owner | hard | The listing must stay with the person who consented |
| The listing is the one this onboarding created | hard | Never "reuse" an existing listing for an onboarding |
| Photos present (cover + at least one gallery photo) | soft | Take real, current photos |
| Opening hours present | soft | Ask and enter them |
| Pin inside the territory, member position near the pin (offline) | soft / info | Stand at the business when you submit |
| Older duplicate exists | soft | Should have been caught in the duplicate step; an admin decides |
| Lead verified (not a self-review) | hard | System-enforced |

## Listing quality

- **Name** exactly as on the signage. No "Best …" or added keywords.
- **Category** the closest real one; never pick a category to make a listing appear elsewhere.
- **Pin** on the entrance, not the street corner; check it in the map preview.
- **Phone / WhatsApp** the business's, confirmed by the owner (the OTP number is not necessarily the business line).
- **Hours** as told by the owner, including closed days.
- **Description** short, factual, in the owner's words. No prices you did not confirm.

## Photos (public)

Landscape, in focus, daylight or good lighting, the business as it is today. Cover: the storefront or the signature view. Gallery: interior, products/menu, a distinguishing feature. No people's faces without their agreement; no children; no other businesses' branding. Upload only photos you took or the owner gave you for this purpose.

## Evidence (private, offline only)

Storefront photo showing signage; interior photo showing the premises; taken **at submission time** with location on (GPS and accuracy are attached). These are never public and exist to prove you were there. Do not reuse photos between businesses.

## Consent

Only the owner's own phone or a consent link opened on the owner's own phone. If the owner is not present and cannot be reached, **do not** proceed — come back or use the online consent link.

## Content (creators)

Truthful, consented, on-brief; figures reported honestly and labelled self-reported by the system; one submission per post.

## What gets you rejected or removed from the team

Fabricated businesses, reused photos, consent codes entered by anyone but the owner, onboarding a listing you or a colleague owns, inflating figures, sharing owners' phone numbers. See [conduct-privacy-security.md](conduct-privacy-security.md).
