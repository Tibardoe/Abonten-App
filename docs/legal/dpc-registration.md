# A2 — Data Protection Commission registration status

**STATUS: VERIFIED — REGISTERED (provisional certificate), 2026-09-17**

The founder supplied two documents issued by the Data Protection Commission on 16 September 2026: a **Certificate of Registration** and a covering **Provisional Certificate Notice**. The notice explains that the Commission issued a provisional certificate because its online registration portal was down, that the organisation "has satisfied the applicable registration and/or renewal requirements and has been duly approved for certification", that the provisional certificate "shall serve as official evidence of the organisation's certification status", and that an electronically generated certificate will be issued through the portal once it is restored and will supersede it. The documents themselves are held by the founder and are **not** committed to the repository.
